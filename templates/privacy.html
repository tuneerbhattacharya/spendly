Privacy Policy

How Spendly collects, uses and protects your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you log, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card numbers or bank login details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to store and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in the Spendly database and kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, and the related data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about your data, please contact the Spendly team at the email address provided on our website.
